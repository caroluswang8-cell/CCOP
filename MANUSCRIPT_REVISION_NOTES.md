# Manuscript revision notes

## Revision objective

The manuscript was rebuilt around the evidence that directly supports the
configuration-assignment theory. Its final numerical sequence is:

1. affine separation of terminal closure and pressure-action placement;
2. long-time accumulation against a same-discretization ISPH star--star
   workflow;
3. strictly nonaffine differential-vortex verification of geometry,
   full-operator transport, the cross response, and complete evolution.

Case 2 now compares only the matched classical ISPH workflow
`D_star^q G_star` with the complete CCOP architecture `D_T^q G_1/2` in the
main text.  The terminal--terminal development control is no longer used to
repeat the target/action ablation already completed by Case 1.

## Green-compatibility clarification

- The full-space value `0.2484` is now defined explicitly as
  `||D_T^q-D_T^ad||_F/||D_T^q||_F`, with
  `D_T^ad=-M_Z^{-1}G_T^T M_U`.
- The manuscript no longer presents this value as an approximation error for
  divergence, pressure action, the pressure solve, or projector algebra.

  Its Riesz-normalized mismatch is `2.26e-16`, while the full-space pair remains
  nonadjoint.

  not substituted for the pressure-test pair used by the method.

## Local-branch theory repair

- The zero-step CCOP residual is now treated as a degenerate equation:
  `R(p,0)=0` for every pressure coefficient when the incoming represented
  constraint is exact.  The manuscript no longer infers a numerical pressure
  expansion directly from invertibility of `D_n G_n`.
- A new rescaled local-branch lemma introduces
  `x_T=x_n+tau u_n+tau^2 xi`, divides the terminal constraint by `tau`, and
  uses Hadamard extension plus the implicit-function theorem.  Its block
  Jacobian is invertible exactly when the gauge-reduced response
  `D_n G_n:Q_{h,0}->Z_h` is bijective.
- The lemma establishes a unique local numerical branch and derives
  `p_Theta(0)=p_n` for a matching configuration-force predictor.  Placement
  enters only in the next branch coefficient.
- The subsequent local-defect proposition proves the midpoint cancellation
  for the force-free predictor and for the second-order implicit-midpoint
  conservative-force predictor used in Cases 2 and 3.  The force-predictor
  expansion is shown explicitly.
- The result is stated at fixed spatial discretization.  No global temporal
  theorem, mesh-uniform implicit-function neighborhood, material CFL law, or
  extension to velocity-dependent viscosity is claimed.
- The exact-start condition remains an analytic hypothesis.  Floating-point
  residuals enter the rescaled equation as a numerical perturbation; no
  `tau >= C epsilon` existence rule is asserted.

## Geometric placement and pressure-work revision

- The abstract and introduction now define $\Theta$ as a geometric operator
  placement, not merely a temporal stage.  Midpoint CCOP is identified as the
  mixed response $D_TG_{1/2}$ rather than the common-stage projection
  $D_{1/2}G_{1/2}$.
- A named discrete pressure work--energy proposition rewrites the existing
  quadratic energy increment as
  $-\Delta t\langle (u^\star+u_T)/2,G_\Theta p_\Theta\rangle_m$ and proves the
  identity by polarization.
- The manuscript now reserves “exact discrete work consistency” for equality
  between the represented pressure work and its induced kinetic-energy change.
  Accuracy relative to the continuous pressure-work integral is treated as a
  separate question governed by action placement, coefficient-stage
  consistency, the predictor, and force or viscous splitting.
- The local midpoint result is described as cancellation of the leading
  configuration-induced velocity defect.  The one-point impulse estimate is
  retained as supporting accuracy analysis rather than presented as the
  source of work consistency.
- Case 1 and the conclusion now state the evidence chain directly: all
  placements satisfy the same exact work identity, while $G_\Theta$ selects
  which finite-step work is represented and $D_T$ selects the terminal
  pressure-test target.
